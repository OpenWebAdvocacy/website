We’re having an OWA meetup in London on **1 October 2026, from 19:00–21:00 BST**, together with [London Web Standards](https://londonwebstandards.org/).

Join us for talks on **browser competition, web apps, browser choice and data portability**, followed by Q&A and a chance to meet people from OWA and others working on these issues.

The event will be held at [Civo Tech Junction, First Floor, 32–37 Cowper St, London EC2A 4AW](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Civo+Tech+Junction/@51.5254771,-0.0884712,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x48761d1107173049:0x60e02a16bed98fdf!8m2!3d51.5254771!4d-0.0858963!16s%2Fg%2F11y3x0zc8z).

Everyone is welcome, but places are limited, so [please book in advance](https://londonwebstandards.org/events/lws-owa-oct-2026/).

      users still don’t have them. Where are they? And when are they actually coming?
    </p>

    <p>
      <strong>Web Apps:</strong>
